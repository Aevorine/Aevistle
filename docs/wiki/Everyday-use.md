## Create a reminder

Choose the sending account, recipients, subject and content. Add attachments if needed, review the time, and schedule the reminder. For a recurring reminder, choose its recurrence rule rather than creating many separate copies.

| Setting | What to review |
|---|---|
| One-off or recurring | Whether the next occurrence matches your intention |
| Recipients | To, Cc and Bcc before scheduling or sending |
| Attachments | Correct files and the provider's size limit |
| Working-day policy | Whether this reminder follows the working calendar |
| Quiet hours and recipient window | Whether rules defer the intended time |
| Catch-up policy | What happens after sleep, shutdown or missed occurrences |

## Change an existing schedule

Use the reminder controls to pause, resume, edit or delete a task. Review the displayed next occurrence after editing a recurring rule. Changes to a repeating reminder can affect future occurrences, rather than just one date.

## Receiving mail

Incoming mail is optional and uses the account's IMAP settings. Check authentication and background permissions if mail only appears after reopening the app. Attachments and message content are private even when account passwords are not present in a log or export.

## Activity and delivery

Check the activity log for the actual send result and provider error. A queued reminder, a successful connection test, server acceptance and arrival in the recipient's inbox are different stages. Inspect the result before manually retrying a message.

See [Working calendar](Working-calendar.md), [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md) and the [feature reference](https://github.com/Aevorine/Aevistle/blob/main/docs/FEATURES.md) for the available scheduling and mail tools.
